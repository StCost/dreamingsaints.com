01 — The creed

Imagination is a material. We keep it on the bench.

Dreaming Saints started as a team gathered around a VR game we loved, BONEWORKS, and the wish to climb with our own hands. The first proper game was RISING BONEvR, a physics puzzle about leaving hell without legs. The demo arrived 7 November 2021. The levels were rough. The lesson stayed. The dream on the bench now is COLLAPSE MACHINE, in work since September 2023.

Between the long work, smaller dreams ship. Whomers Ate My Lawn! was made in a month and released 6 April 2025. Ball-Aqua put a free demo on Steam on 20 August 2026, with the full game set for 25 February 2027.

The house rule, written for Emporia and true of the workshop: things you wear and things you make for the joy of making are infinite. Things that wound, heal, or move you are finite. Spend them like they matter.

I · In the workshop since Sep 2023

COLLAPSE MACHINE

Tesla-punk co-op immersive sim

Scavenge, raid, and keep a moving home on Emporia, a super-magnetic wasteland. Walk the cargo hold while a friend drives the bunker truck. Dig voxel earth. Hoard food, metal, medicine, and tesla-punk gear that has to physically fit the box. Swap bodies when the one you have fails. The planet remembers the cracked rock, the moved plant, the half-used pill.

StCost, on the first picture: walk in co-op inside the hangar while someone else drives over the dunes. The camera moves. The physics inside stays put.

Action, adventure, RPG, simulation · single-player, online and LAN co-op · thirty languages · release to be announced

II · Demo 20 Aug 2026 full game 25 Feb 2027

Ball-Aqua

Tropical ball-rolling multiplayer

A speedy physics ball in the lineage of Sonic Adventure and Ballance. Spin Dash, grind rails, and keep the speed through grass islands, beaches, glass towers, aquariums, and airships. Glossy water below. Dolphins in the sky when you jump. Freeroam alone or in co-op, then Race, King of the Hill, and Golf. Build a course and put it on the Steam Workshop. Paint the ball. Clear a stage to the last star.

The spark was a Frutiger Aero water album, Alezya’s Dream Island, and a dislike of ball games that only hop along thin beams.

Action, racing, casual · achievements, leaderboards, workshop · thirty-one languages · free demo

III · Released 6 Apr 2025 $0.99

Whomers Ate My Lawn!

A farm of tiny pets

A 64×64 lawn, sixty-four layers deep. Whomers dig, eat, and lay eggs. Food becomes bushes. Predators hunt the eggs. Golems chew boulders. You play as a small god: left click digs, right click restores, and anything loose can be dragged. Grow a colony from one egg toward a thousand, or scrape the grass clean. Made in MonoGame, in a month, because a RimWorld-shaped wish wanted a quieter toy.

Casual simulation · single-player · Steam achievements and cloud · Windows

IV · Demo 7 Nov 2021 the first game

RISING BONEvR

A physics climb out of hell

You wake at the bottom. Each circle is a level, and the way up is a body that has arms and no legs. Doors can be keyed, locks broken, windows smashed, floors ducked. Charon takes you onward if you earn the height. Secrets, a little of the story, and the question of whether the life above is worth the climb. VR only. The first real C# game in the workshop, and the first Blender models StTaya ever made.

StCost, looking back: the physics was a lesson in vectors, the levels were bad, and it was fun when it worked. Someday, with what we know now.

Action, adventure · single-player · SteamVR · English · coming, the first circle already open

03 — La bottega

Three saints. The St. is the name.

Constantine signs the work as StCost. The workshop is him, StTaya, and StGrikus. The long shelves of favourite games, songs, and tools are kept in About Us.

S.

StCost

Team lead · software · design

Constantine. A PC of his own in 2011, then TF2 and Minecraft servers, then C++ terminals at university in 2016, then a Mad Max survival role-play server that filled every slot. He builds the games and writes the design of Emporia. Skateboard, FPV, Earl Grey. The house orange is his, and it is not a suggestion: #ff8000.

StTaya

3D · art direction · animation · levels

Fiancée, and the first sculptor in the room. Her Blender models gave RISING BONEvR a body. She skates, breakdances, boxes, kickboxes, and camps. The second house colour sits with her, the tiffany of the engagement ring in the chronicle: #0abab5.
