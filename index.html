COLLAPSE MACHINE

Tesla-punk co-op open-world immersive sim

Scavenge, raid, and survive on Emporia, a super-magnetic wasteland. Hoard tesla-punk gear that has to physically fit the box. Mine voxel terrain. Run a bunker truck you can walk inside while someone else drives, or take a buggy, a hoverboard, or a zeppelin. The world remembers cracked rock, moved plants, and half-used pills. Swap bodies when the one you have fails.

Action, adventure, RPG, simulation. Single-player, online co-op, and LAN. Thirty languages. Release to be announced.

Ball-Aqua

Tropical ball-rolling multiplayer

A speedy physics ball in the line of Sonic Adventure and Ballance. Spin Dash, grind rails, and keep the speed through grass islands, beaches, glass towers, aquariums, and airships. Play freeroam alone or in online co-op, then Race, King of the Hill, and Golf. Build a course, publish it on the Steam Workshop, and paint the ball. Clear a stage to the last star.

Action, racing, casual. Achievements, leaderboards, and a level editor. Thirty-one languages. Free demo.

Whomers Ate My Lawn!

A lawn of tiny pets

A 64 by 64 lawn, sixty-four layers deep. Whomers dig, eat, and lay eggs. Food becomes bushes. Predators hunt the eggs. Golems chew boulders. Left click digs, right click restores, and anything loose can be dragged. Grow a colony from one egg toward a thousand, or scrape the grass clean. Made in MonoGame.

Casual simulation. Single-player. Steam achievements and cloud. Windows.

RISING BONEvR

A physics climb out of hell

You wake at the bottom. Each circle of hell is a level, and the way up is a body with arms and no legs. A door can be keyed, a lock broken, a window smashed, a floor ducked. Charon takes you onward if you earn the height. VR only, played through SteamVR. The first level is the open demo.

Action, adventure. Single-player. English. Full game still coming.
